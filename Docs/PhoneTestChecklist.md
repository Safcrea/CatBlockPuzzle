# Phone test checklist

Tick these off on a real phone (and, if you can, a tablet). Editor screenshots cannot tell you how it feels.

## Cat shelf
- [ ] Level 5 and a level with a 4-tall cat: cats sit fully inside their cards, also while bobbing.
- [ ] Drag a cat out and drop it back: it lands inside the card, no cat pokes out during the pop-in.
- [ ] A level with "+N cats waiting": the pill still sits on the shelf edge and does not cover a card.
- [ ] Nothing overlaps: board, shelf, power-up row, bottom cat decoration. Try the tallest and the widest phone you own.

## Hammer (one tap clears the way)
- [ ] Below level 8 the Hammer is grey with `Lv 8`; tapping it shows the "Unlocks at level 8" popup. Buttons read Hint, Hammer, Freeze (unlock order).
- [ ] Reaching level 8 shows the Hammer tutorial once, hand on the button.
- [ ] Place a cat on another cat's spot, tap Hammer: it swings, the blocking cat flies back to the shelf, count drops by one. A cat standing on its own spot is never touched.
- [ ] Tap Hammer with nothing in the way: "Nothing in the way! You're on track.", error sound, count unchanged.
- [ ] A padlock level: Hammer breaks the padlocks that keep a waiting cat out (lock-break sound, no chest sound); up to 3 at a time.
- [ ] A fog level: Hammer blows all the fog away.
- [ ] Pause or leave the level mid-swing: nothing is left drawn on the board and the board is playable again.
- [ ] Out of Hammers with something in the way: tapping opens the purchase popup; buying works.
- [ ] Sound off silences the Hammer sounds.

## Hints near the end
- [ ] With one or two cats left and a wrong cat sitting on their spot: Hint still glows the spot and pulses the cat in the way. It never does nothing while the puzzle is unsolved.

## Stuck pointer (no popups)
- [ ] After 12 s idle (or 3 wrong drops): only a glow and a tapping hand on the right power-up button. No toast, no popup. Any touch removes it.

## Hearts
- [ ] First level with hearts: no card stops play; the hearts glow with a tapping hand for a few seconds.
- [ ] Losing a heart: pink shards burst off it, the row shakes. With one heart left it beats until the level ends.

## Sound and music
- [ ] Every tap plays on finger lift, in step with the action (nothing plays first).
- [ ] Sound off stays off after restarting the app.

## Daily reward
- [ ] First launch: no timer on the first open, Claim works on a manual open.
- [ ] Claim, close, reopen: countdown shows and is aligned under the day label.

## Screens and resolutions
- [ ] Portrait only, safe area respected on a notched phone, nothing under the notch or the gesture bar.
- [ ] Home, Levels, Shop, Settings, Pause, Win, Fail popups: background dimmed so the popup is the focus.
- [ ] Tablet: board and shelf scale, no stretched art, buttons still reachable.

## Dumb-player pass
- [ ] Play 10 minutes the lazy way: random drops, mash every button, background and foreground the app mid-level, rotate the phone, receive a call. Nothing freezes, no black screen, no stuck input.

## Stuck nudge
- [ ] From level 4, stand idle for 12 s (or drop a cat in a wrong place 3 times): the Hint button glows with a tapping hand and a message. Touching anything removes it.
- [ ] It does not appear during a popup, a tutorial, pause or while the Hammer is armed, and not more than 3 times per level.
- [ ] Levels 1-3 (no Hint yet): stand idle 12 s and the next cat plus its spot glow with "Try this cat in the glowing spot!".
- [ ] Hammer tutorial at level 8: tapping the Hammer on the empty board shows "Hammer ready!" instead of doing nothing.
- [ ] After level 8, the fog and padlock intro cards mention the Hammer.

## Rewards, shop and Level 1
- [ ] Daily Rewards: Day 5 shows a hammer with "x2"; claiming it on that day adds 2 Hammers (HUD count after level 8, shop/popup).
- [ ] Shop: the starter bundle lists 1 Hammer and buying it adds Hammers.
- [ ] Hammer sounds: arm, strike thud, knocked-cat meow, back sound when you put it away; sound off silences all of them.
- [ ] Level 1 (clear the tutorial save first): the card above the board reads "Give this cat a home / Drag the glowing cat onto the green spaces." Text is sharp, nothing overlaps the board or the shelf. Step 2 and the "Green means it fits!" line while dragging look the same.
- [ ] "Placed cats can move!" tip bubble is readable and stays on screen when the cat is at the edge.

## Shop, polish and Settings "?"
- [ ] Shop: Hint, Freeze and Hammer cards sit in one row, text readable, Buy buttons all tappable; buying a Hammer adds one (count on the HUD after level 8); with too few coins you get "Not enough coins".
- [ ] Starter bundle line reads in full, no "...".
- [ ] Use Hint, Freeze and Hammer: the button squashes and sparks fly; Freeze sprays ice over the timer; Hint sparks on the pointed spot. With Reduced Motion on, none of that moves.
- [ ] Hint and Freeze unlock tutorials (levels 4 and 13): the big icon is sharp, not blurry.
- [ ] Settings: the blue "?" button sits left of the red close button, taps it, popup says "Tutorials are back!"; then level 1 shows its walkthrough again and new power-ups re-introduce themselves.

## Hammer sounds and Sleeping cats
- [ ] Arm the Hammer: a spring wind-up. Strike: a short crunch exactly on contact. Smash a cat or fog: a bigger break. Smash a padlock: the lock break (no chest-open sound on top).
- [ ] Sound off silences all four.
- [ ] A level with stone cells shows "Sleeping cats!" once; the Settings "?" brings it back.

## Round 5: tutorials, Hammer need, hints
- [ ] Level 4 (clear tutorial save first): the screen dims except the Hint button, a ring pulses, the button bounces, "STUCK? TAP HINT!" and a hand poking it. Tapping anywhere else does nothing; tapping the button ends it and then shows a real hint. Same look for Freeze on level 13 and the clock on level 11.
- [ ] Level 1: bold white outlined words above the board (no card), still readable on a bright background.
- [ ] Hammer: it is grey ("Lv 16") until level 16. Level 16 (first padlock) opens with the spotlight on the Hammer, "BREAK THE PADLOCK! TAP HAMMER!", you get 3 free Hammers, and tapping it swings and opens the padlock (2 left). Levels before 16 never show it.
- [ ] Hammer clears every wrong cat (up to 5), then the next cat and spot pulse (free hint). It never touches a cat on its own spot.
- [ ] Hint on every level 14 and above: one tap uses one hint, never a purchase popup while you still own one.
- [ ] Hint after a few wrong drops, on levels with padlocks (for example 24, 44, 99): the pulse always points at something you can actually do.
